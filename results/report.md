# SG-Transit Reliability Experiments

Generated by `python -m experiments.run_all` using `plan()` for strategy selection and `evaluate()` for held-out simulation.

Selection samples are separate from the 10,000 held-out samples. Results compare synthetic baselines and do not claim to beat Google Maps.

## Scenarios

| Scenario | Recommended | Routes | Mean | P95 | Mean/tail conflicts |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| single_critical_transfer | reliability_policy | 2 | 54.51 | 64.88 | 0 |
| alternative_services | reliability_policy | 2 | 44.47 | 53.68 | 0 |
| transfer_tradeoff | reliability_policy | 2 | 34.88 | 40.58 | 3 |

A conflict means one evaluated strategy has a lower mean but a higher P95 than another. A zero count is a valid result; no threshold or hand-picked outcome is used.

Each scenario also includes a phase_correlation=1 pressure comparison to test whether a multi-service advantage depends on independent timing.
